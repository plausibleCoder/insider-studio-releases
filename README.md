# Insider Studio

**A private, all-in-one toolbox for your Windows laptop.** Notes, calendar and tasks, a resume builder, PDF editing,
voice, images and more, in one app. **Everything runs on your computer: your notes, resumes and files never leave it.**
No account, no subscription, no cloud.

> This is an early preview shared for feedback. Thank you for trying it!

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**: under *Assets*, click `InsiderStudio-Setup-<version>.exe` (about 370 MB).

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 1 GB of free disk space. No administrator rights needed.

## Install (2 minutes)

1. Open the downloaded `InsiderStudio-Setup-….exe`.
2. Windows may show a blue box, **"Windows protected your PC"**. That's because this preview isn't paid-signed yet.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Next** through the steps. Keep "Create a desktop shortcut" ticked if you'd like one.
4. Click **Finish**. Insider Studio opens.

Afterwards, open it any time from the **desktop shortcut** or the **Start menu** ("Insider Studio").

## What's inside

| | |
|---|---|
| **Notes** | Notebooks, sections and pages with a Notion-style editor, maths, and drawing boards for diagrams |
| **Calendar & Tasks** | Day, week, month and year views, smart task lists, priorities and tags |
| **Resume Builder** | 10 templates with sample content, folders, versions, PDF and Word export |
| **PDF Editor & Toolkit** | Edit the text already in a PDF, find & replace, sign, crop, merge, split, compress, protect |
| **Voice** | Text to speech with offline voices, voice recorder, audio toolkit |
| **Images & video** | Converter, background remover, video toolkit, screen recorder, graphic maker, QR codes |
| **Text tools** | Word and token counter, compare and merge texts, JSON, encoding, hashes |

*Presenter videos (AI talking-head) aren't in this edition: they need a large graphics card.*

## Your privacy

- Your files are processed on your computer and saved in **Documents\Insider Studio**.
- Your notes, calendar and resumes are kept in **%APPDATA%\Insider Studio**, safe across updates.
- A few **optional** features use the internet, and each is labelled. They are: Google sync, the online voices, the AI suggestions in the resume builder (with your own key), and a one-time download of the background-removal model (~170 MB) the first time you use it.
- **Settings → Privacy → Offline only** switches every one of them off.

## Updating

Download the newer `InsiderStudio-Setup-….exe` and run it. It installs over the old version and keeps all your notes and files.

## Uninstalling

**Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Insider Studio → Uninstall.** Your notes and files stay where they are,
in case you come back. Delete the folders above if you want them gone too.
